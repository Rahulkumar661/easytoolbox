What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of many goods and services.

How to Calculate GST?

To calculate GST, enter the original amount and the applicable GST rate in the calculator above.

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Total Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

Common GST Rates

  • 5% GST
  • 12% GST
  • 18% GST
  • 28% GST

The applicable GST rate depends on the particular goods or services and the applicable tax rules.
