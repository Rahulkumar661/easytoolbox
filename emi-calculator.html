What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount that a borrower generally pays every month towards a loan until the loan is fully repaid.

How is EMI Calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

The standard EMI formula is:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ⁿ / ((1 + R)ⁿ - 1)

Here, P is the loan principal, R is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly instalments.
