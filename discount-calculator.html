What is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction in the original price of a product or service. Discounts are commonly offered during sales, promotions, festivals and special offers.

A discount calculator helps you quickly find how much money you save and how much you need to pay after applying the discount.

How to Calculate Discount?

The discount amount can be calculated using the following formula:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final price after discount is:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

For example, if a product costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount amount is ₹200 and the final price is ₹800.

Discount Calculator Example

Suppose the original price of a product is ₹2,000 and the discount is 15%.

  • Original Price = ₹2,000
  • Discount = 15%
  • Discount Amount = ₹300
  • Final Price = ₹1,700

So, you save ₹300 and pay ₹1,700 after the discount.
