What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth to the current date. It can show the result in years, months and days.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth in the calculator above and click the "Calculate Age" button. The calculator will automatically calculate your age.
