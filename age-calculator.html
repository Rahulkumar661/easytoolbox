What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth to the current date.

How to Calculate Your Age

Select your date of birth above and click the "Calculate Age" button. The calculator will show your age in years, months and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

  • Calculate your exact age quickly.
  • Easy to use on mobile and computer.
  • No registration required.
  • Free to use.
