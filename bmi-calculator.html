What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measure that relates body weight to height.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, if a person's weight is 60 kg and height is 1.65 metres, their BMI is approximately 22.0.

BMI Categories

  • Underweight: BMI below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: BMI 18.5–24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25–29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30 or above

These categories are general adult screening ranges. BMI does not directly measure body fat and may not be appropriate for everyone, including children and some athletes.
